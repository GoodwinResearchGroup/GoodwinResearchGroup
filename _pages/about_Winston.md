Winston is the Director of Rest and Relaxation for the Goodwin Research Group at UNSW Canberra, specialising in low-energy ground states and long-term conformational stability (snoozing).
He has perfected the art of doing very little, where his primary research focuses on guarding his favourite teddy bear and barking at kangaroos nearby. At UNSW, he continues to set the standard for work–life balance, conserving his energy for friendly greetings and tail wags to anyone passing by.
