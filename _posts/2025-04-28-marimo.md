We are thrilled to officially launch the **Goodwin Research Group**! This marks the start of an exciting new chapter as we set up our lab and begin exploring the fascinating world of photo-switching supramolecular molecules and host-guest chemistry.

### What We Are Building
Our group is dedicated to designing and studying complex molecular architectures and non-covalent interactions. Over the coming months, we will be:
* **Setting Up the Lab:** Unpacking instruments, configuring our synthetic workspaces, and running our very first reactions. Woohoo!!!!
* **Growing the Team:** Welcoming our inaugural Honours, PhD, and undergraduate researchers to the group.
* **Exploring New Frontiers:** Investigating novel host-guest systems and functional supramolecular materials.

### Stay Connected
We will be using this **News** space to share lab milestones, new publications, conference highlights, and research opportunities as our group grows. 

If you are a prospective student, postdoctoral researcher, or collaborator interested in what we do, please feel free to explore our [Projects](/projects/) and [Publications](/publications/), or reach out via email to chat about joining the team!
